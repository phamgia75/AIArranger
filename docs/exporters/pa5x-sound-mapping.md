# Pa5X sound mapping: corpus report and fill-in guide

**Verified Pa5X sound mappings: 0 of 32 Korg tracks** (4 styles × 8 tracks, fed by 34 exported Genos parts; 2 tracks merge two parts).
Every sample map leaves every track `unresolved`. No Pa5X Bank MSB, Bank LSB or Program Change number appears anywhere in this repo. Those numbers come only from the instrument.

Sample maps (generated by `aiarranger-convert --sound-template`):
`sound-maps/POP_ACOUSTIC_2.soundmap`, `sound-maps/CLASSIC_6_8.soundmap`,
`sound-maps/BALLAD_FOLK.soundmap`, `sound-maps/C_WHISPER.soundmap`.

## 1. Schema (`--sound-map`)

```
# comment
<track> <msb 0-127> <lsb 0-127> <pc 0-127>     # verified on Pa5X
<track> unresolved                             # looked at, no verified sound yet
```

- `<track>` is one of `bass drum perc acc1 acc2 acc3 acc4 acc5`.
- Each track may appear at most once.
- A number with anything attached, a value above 127, a missing field or an extra field rejects the whole file. The CLI then exits with code 1 and writes no output.
- An `unresolved` line emits nothing, so the output is byte-identical to a conversion run without a map.
- For a verified line, the converter writes CC0 = msb, CC32 = lsb and PC = pc at tick 0 of every element. Korg requires them at tick 0.

## 2. Family classification

The family hint is assigned in this order:

1. The Korg target track: Drum becomes **Drum Kit** and Percussion becomes **Percussion**.
2. An instrument keyword in the Genos part name: Piano/EP, Organ, Guitar, Strings, Brass, Synth, Pad.
3. The track role: Pad or Bass.
4. Otherwise **Unidentified**.

The Yamaha voice number is *not* used to infer a family. Genos banks (MSB 8, 63, 104, 127, …) are not GM, so a program number alone does not identify an instrument.

## 4. MegaVoice and articulations that do not carry over

A Yamaha MegaVoice plays different articulations (mute, slide, noise) depending on velocity zone and key range. No Pa5X sound reproduces that layout. The converter flags a part when it shows a **strong** sign:

- notes in C-1..B-1, which are dropped because Pa5X reads them as chord events, or
- notes at C7 or above on a Guitar, Bass or Unidentified part.

A velocity spread across the Mega zones 1-64, 65-90, 91-100 and 101-127 is listed only as supporting evidence. On its own it is just normal dynamics.

| POP_ACOUSTIC_2 | ch 16 Part14 (63/120/51) | 96 notes in C-1..B-1 **dropped**; 4 zones |

Several flagged Chord parts use bank 63/120. That pattern is an observation, not a verified MegaVoice identification. For each flagged part, pick a Pa5X sound and check by ear. If the high or low keys produce unwanted noises, choose a sound without FX keys, or mute the part in that element on Pa5X.

## 5. Drum notes outside GM 35..81

Drum notes pass through unchanged and are never transposed. Notes outside the GM percussion range are kept and counted. Whether a Pa5X kit has a sound on those keys is **unverified**.

The corpus kits (Yamaha 127/0 and 62/100) play keys 13-34, below the GM map. What those keys sound like on Genos is not documented in this repo. On Pa5X, choose a kit and listen for missing hits. Missing ones need a kit with sounds on those keys, or a note remap on Pa5X. The repo has no translation table and will not invent one.

## 6. Mappings to confirm on Pa5X

All 32 Korg tracks need a sound choice, and every choice needs confirming by ear. Priority order:

1. **Drum and Percussion**: the kit choice decides whether the non-GM keys in §5 sound at all.
2. **Bass**: on all 4 styles.
3. **Merged tracks**: POP Acc4 (E.P rt + E.Piano) and BALLAD Acc5 (Tbn Bs + Tbn). Each is two Genos parts on one Korg track with one sound. Check that both lines still make sense.
4. The **MegaVoice candidates** in §4.
5. The remaining **Unidentified** parts. Listen to the part solo on Pa5X first, then choose a family.

## 7. Filling in a sound map from the Pa5X

1. Convert and generate the template:
   `aiarranger-convert --input X.sty --target pa5x --output X.mid --sound-template X.soundmap`
2. Import `X.mid` on Pa5X (see `pa5x-hardware-validation.md` §1). Select each track and pick a sound by ear in the Sound Select window, using the family comment in the template as a starting point.
3. Read that sound's **Bank MSB, Bank LSB and Program Change** from the Pa5X itself: the sound's info in the Sound Select window, or the Pa5X MIDI implementation / sound list in the official Korg documentation for the OS version installed. Do not use a number you have not seen on the instrument or in the official Korg list, and do not copy the Yamaha numbers from the comments.
4. Replace `unresolved` with the three numbers, for example `bass 121 0 33` (illustrative only, not a verified Pa5X sound). Leave the other lines `unresolved`.
5. Re-run with `--sound-map X.soundmap`. The report shows `MSB .. LSB .. PC .. (user map)` for that track.
6. Re-import on Pa5X and confirm the track now loads the intended sound. Only then commit the line, with a comment naming the Pa5X OS version.
